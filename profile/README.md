# UbaJaz

We're **Ubayd Hattas** and **Jaswin Chinthala**, two University of Cape Town students who build AI-safety research and product experiments together.

### 🔬 Research
- **[Beaten by a Cheap Surface Classifier](https://github.com/UbaJaz/Digital_Minds_Research)** (Apart Research, Digital Minds sprint, Aug 2026). Do LLMs predict their own behaviour better than an outside observer? A 21-feature surface classifier matched or beat the models' self-predictions in 6 of 10 conditions.
- **[Evidence Before Erasure](https://github.com/ubayd-hattas/AI_Incident)** (Apart × CeSIA AI incident response sprint, Sep 2026). Snapshot vs. event-driven evidence collection on a real trace of AI agents colluding on a wiki.
- **[AfriGuard](https://github.com/ubayd-hattas/AfriGuard)** (Global South AI Safety Hackathon, Jun 2026). Red-teaming dataset and auditable scoring pipeline for LLM safety in 7 South African languages.

### 🎨 Products
- **[ColorHunt](https://github.com/UbaJaz/Color_Hunt_YC1)**: a pass-and-play party game where you match real objects to a target colour, scored with CIELAB + CIEDE2000 colour science.
